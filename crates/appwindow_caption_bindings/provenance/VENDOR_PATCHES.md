All patches are opt-in proof scaffolding on branch `test/windows-appwindow-lifecycle`,
gated by `SNAFU_WINDOWS_APPWINDOW_CAPTION_PROOF=1` (unset behavior is unchanged).
Owner removes these together with the proof after root's native acceptance, or
when the proof is rejected.
